# The Long Root Express

An abandoned biological transit line crosses a distance that would otherwise take days. It begins as a cautious burrow tutorial and becomes exhilarating fast travel, but the system has grown around its old safety mechanisms. The area tells a small story about infrastructure becoming habitat.

Six rooms form a mostly linear run with one maintenance offshoot and a shortcut back to the station. Visual language matters: broad glowing roots mean speed, thin dull roots mean control, and exposed knots mean a place where the player can safely leave the line.

## 1. Platform Zero

**Mood:** Calm expectation—the silence before departure.

**Combination:** SavePoint, InspectPoint, Wildlife, and a short `SpeedScale: 1` BurrowLine.

The line is too short to be exciting. Its purpose is to establish the station markings and let the player compare ordinary burrow speed with what follows.

**Wrinkle:** Small insects travel along the outside of the root in the opposite direction. They are decoration now, but foreshadow that later tunnels are shared ecosystems rather than sterile tubes.

An InspectPoint reads: “EXPRESS ROOT. DO NOT SURFACE BETWEEN MARKERS.”

## 2. The Merge Ramp

**Mood:** Gathering momentum.

**Combination:** `SpeedScale: 1.6` BurrowLine, Dropper, and a directional Bouncer at an exposed knot.

The Dropper makes standing still outside the line undesirable. The bouncer catches the player after the first fast exit and returns them to the next segment.

**Wrinkle:** Following the line to its literal end is safe but slow. Surfacing one marker early preserves momentum into the bouncer and produces a much longer launch. The room introduces fast travel as something expressive, not merely automatic.

## 3. Live Junction

**Mood:** Choice under visible electrical pressure.

**Combination:** Two BurrowLines with different SpeedScale values, LightningTraps, and reflective metal slopes.

The slow line winds around the electrical apparatus. The express line passes directly between its timed arcs.

**Wrinkle:** Reflections from the metal slopes reveal that the fast line's danger windows are actually shorter in real time: speed is protection if the player commits. Hesitating at a junction is more dangerous than choosing either route cleanly.

## 4. Service Interruption

**Mood:** Intimate, dark, and exploratory after the speed of the junction.

**Combination:** CameraBlocker, RevealDoor, Brambles, and a mycelon Chest.

The express root appears severed. A cramped maintenance channel behind the wall provides the only continuation.

**Wrinkle:** The hidden route curls behind the broken line and reveals that Brambles caused the failure by growing toward its warmth. Cutting through to the chest also uncovers a repair knot that reconnects the room into a permanent shortcut. The optional reward and route repair are the same discovery.

## 5. Shared Tunnel

**Mood:** A brief, controlled chase.

**Combination:** `SpeedScale: 2.4` BurrowLine, Mosquitoes, Watcher projectiles, and alternating surface shelters.

Enemies cannot reach the player inside the line, but they concentrate fire on the exposed knots. Each shelter permits a moment to read the next segment.

**Wrinkle:** Projectiles continue traveling while the player is underground. Entering a segment immediately can cause old shots to meet the player at its far end. Sometimes the correct response is to surface, bait a volley, then reverse a short distance before taking the express line.

## 6. The Far Terminal

**Mood:** Velocity resolving into height and open air.

**Combination:** `SpeedScale: 3` BurrowLine, Rope attached to a MovingPlatform, and a rare BurrowUpgrade Chest.

The final line launches the player into a large terminal where the moving platform crosses far above the exit. Its rope is the only forgiving object in the landing arc.

**Wrinkle:** Missing the rope does not kill the player; it drops them onto the slow maintenance return discovered in room 4. Catching it preserves speed and reaches the upgrade. The reward recognizes mastery while failure feeds naturally back into the area's topology.
