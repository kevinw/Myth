# The Orchard That Hunts

A cultivation chamber has confused pruning with predation. Its machines still tend the garden, but they increasingly classify the player—and eventually their own wildlife—as unwanted growth. The story moves from abundance to menace, then teaches the player to turn the orchard's reflexes against its keeper.

Six rooms form a bent horseshoe around a sealed seed cellar. Greens and soft golds become bruised magenta and saw-metal grey toward the center. Calm rooms are genuinely calm; the dangerous rooms are short, physical bursts rather than continuous attrition.

## 1. Pollination Porch

**Mood:** Warm, overgrown, and generous.

**Combination:** Plants, Wildlife, gentle Wind, and two Ropes.

The wind makes the ropes breathe while bugs and drifting spores move in the same direction. Crossing is forgiving and primarily establishes the orchard's visual language.

**Wrinkle:** One rope hangs partly outside the Wind volume. It swings strongly until its midpoint, then settles over the landing. The player learns that Wind is spatial rather than a room-wide rule, a fact later rooms can weaponize.

An InspectPoint reads: “WE KEPT WHAT FLOWERED.”

## 2. The Walking Trellis

**Mood:** Curious and toy-like, with no enemies yet.

**Combination:** Ropes attached to vertical MovingPlatforms and radial Bouncers.

Two trellises rise at different speeds. Their hanging ropes cross, while bouncers below recover missed transfers.

**Wrinkle:** The recovery bouncers are also the fastest route. A cautious player climbs rope to rope; an observant player intentionally drops, catches a bouncer at the right phase, and skips half the apparatus. Failure space becomes mastery space.

## 3. Pruner Row

**Mood:** The first sharp break in tone: dry clicking in dense leaves.

**Combination:** ceiling-climbing Spider, Brambles, and Bouncers.

The Spider lays hazards above a narrow path while brambles deny comfortable standing positions. The bouncers initially look like extra chaos.

**Wrinkle:** Bouncers affect the Spider too. Striking one when the Spider crosses its edge knocks the predator away from the ceiling and temporarily clears the route. The player's traversal tool becomes crowd control, but careless timing throws the enemy onto the next landing instead.

## 4. Nectar Crosswind

**Mood:** Bright, exposed, and briefly frantic.

**Combination:** Mosquito projectiles, Wind affecting Projectiles and Enemies, and a central Rope.

The rope offers vertical mobility while a crosswind bends bullets and slowly displaces the Mosquitoes.

**Wrinkle:** Shots fired from the upwind side curve harmlessly into a stone rib; shots from the downwind side become faster and flatter. The safest side of the rope reverses as enemies drift across it. The room asks the player to read relationships, not memorize a pattern.

## 5. The Seed Cellar

**Mood:** Compressed quiet after the crosswind.

**Combination:** Carryable Crate, persistent Button, Door, and a Chest hidden behind a RevealDoor.

The crate holds open a heavy seed-vault door. The visible reward lies beyond it, apparently making this a straightforward pressure-plate puzzle.

**Wrinkle:** Carrying the crate through closes the route behind. Leaving it opens the vault but makes the high return ledge unreachable. The concealed alcove contains a second small crate, discovered by noticing vines growing across “solid” masonry. Exploration converts a forced choice into a complete solution.

## 6. The Orchard Keeper

**Mood:** A compact, theatrical confrontation followed by sudden stillness.

**Combination:** ShieldKnight, circular MovingPlatform, and directional Bouncers.

The keeper patrols a central island while moving branches periodically expose its back. Bouncers provide the height needed to cross its guard.

**Wrinkle:** The bouncers also reposition the ShieldKnight. Launching it onto the orbiting platform creates an easier attack window, but the platform later delivers it to the player's side. After defeat, Wildlife cautiously returns from the room edges and the exit grows quiet rather than showering the player with spectacle.
