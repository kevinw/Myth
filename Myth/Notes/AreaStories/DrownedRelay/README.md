# The Drowned Relay

An old water-distribution station has continued making decisions long after the settlement it served disappeared. The area begins sleepy and almost pastoral, wakes into dangerous industrial motion, then ends with clean water running through machinery that finally seems purposeful again.

The route is a six-room loop. Rooms 1 and 6 overlook one another through grates, so the return feels spatially earned. Blue-green water and warm inactive bulbs gradually give way to white arcs and amber machinery. The rhythm is calm, puzzle, release, danger, chaos, calm.

## 1. The Silt Vestibule

**Mood:** Cool, hushed, and safe. Water noises arrive before any machinery is visible.

**Combination:** Water, Wildlife, and a Rope attached to a slow MovingPlatform.

The first crossing is small enough to study without pressure. Fish follow the platform's shadow while its rope trails through the pool, establishing that attachments inherit motion.

**Wrinkle:** The platform itself passes behind a low ceiling and cannot be ridden all the way. The rope is initially decoration, then becomes the only part of the ferry that clears the obstruction. The player changes from passenger to climber halfway across.

An InspectPoint reads: “INTAKE: ACCEPT WHAT ARRIVES.”

## 2. The Counterweight Sluice

**Mood:** Patient mechanical labor. Nothing attacks, but everything slowly drifts out of place.

**Combination:** Carryable Crate, weight-activated MovingPlatform, and ConveyorBelt.

The crate starts on a belt that carries it away from the lift. Placing either the player or crate on the lift sends it upward, but the useful cargo must eventually arrive with the player.

**Wrinkle:** The first obvious solution strands the crate above. The satisfying solution uses the player's body to start the lift, steps off at the intermediate ledge, then lets the returning platform collect the crate. The room teaches a sequence, not simply “put weight on plate.”

## 3. The Overflow Teeth

**Mood:** Sudden playfulness above something dangerous.

**Combination:** A directional Bouncer attached to a horizontal MovingPlatform, a deep pool, and one Mosquito.

The moving spring is both ferry and launch timing. It carries the player over the water, compresses, and throws toward alternating ledges.

**Wrinkle:** The Mosquito is deliberately positioned so its shots often trigger the player's early evasive jump. Waiting is safer than reacting. On the return route, the same mobile bouncer catches a fall and turns a former hazard into a fast shortcut.

## 4. Ground Fault

**Mood:** Clinical fear: the relay has noticed the player.

**Combination:** LightningTraps, reflective metal slopes, and a BurrowLine beneath the machinery.

Above ground, angled metal makes the lightning pattern legible but demanding. Beneath it, the BurrowLine seems like a complete bypass.

**Wrinkle:** The buried route periodically surfaces at the reflection's safe pockets. The “easy” path therefore becomes a rhythm of hiding below and emerging exactly where the visible lightning geometry already taught safety. A Watcher pressures the surface pauses without reaching the buried player.

## 5. The Pump Heart

**Mood:** Loud, enclosed, and consequential.

**Combination:** Persistent Switch, resizable Door, FloLights, and a short enemy encounter.

The switch sits across the chamber from a sealed spillway. Its authored trail crosses the entire mechanism, making activation a visible journey rather than an abstract click.

**Wrinkle:** The pulse passes through three dormant FloLights. They illuminate in sequence as it reaches them, while shutters close behind the player and the spillway opens ahead. The state change first appears threatening, then reveals that the station is routing water away from the return corridor.

## 6. The Clear Return

**Mood:** Spacious relief. The machinery remains active, but no longer hostile.

**Combination:** Wildlife, RevealDoor, CameraBlocker, and a mycelon Chest.

The new route runs behind the wall glimpsed in room 1. A reveal peels away the masonry and exposes both the chest and a view through to the original pool.

**Wrinkle:** The chest is not the main reward. The opened wall lets the player cross room 1's water in seconds, using the returning ferry rope from the opposite side. Fish now occupy the clean channel, making the changed state readable without text.
