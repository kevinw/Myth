\begin{frame}{Abstract}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \column{0.55\textwidth}
    \small
    We present \emph{Human Questions}, an ancient datacenter dive with a partner in crime:
    an appetizer-sized, knowledge-gated exploration game with juicy combat and
    unconventional traversal mechanics.

    \medskip
    We propose to finish and release the single-player game on Steam by Q2~2027 with a single author ($n=1$)
    and a budget of \$50{,}000.

    \medskip
    Preliminary results from an in-person showcase%
    \footnote[frame]{Showcased at \weblink[Talk \& Play \#57]{https://www.meetup.com/de-de/berlingamescene/events/316056617/} in Berlin.}
    suggest that gliding along walls as a stream of photons is fun ($p<0.05$). One test subject had to be dragged away from the gamepad.

    \medskip
    \textbf{Keywords:} metroidbrainia; burrowing; neural cellular automata; ducks

\begin{frame}{Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \column{0.48\textwidth}
    \textbf{What Stays}
    \footnotesize
    \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.15em}
      \item Dense, intricately connected world demanding attention~[1,\,2]
      \item Mood, mystery, atmosphere, a certain lightness~[3]
      \item Music at the emotional core (composed in-house, $n=1$)
      \item Respect for: player's time and intelligence

    \textbf{Non-goals}
    \footnotesize
    \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.15em}
      \item Hand-drawn vector-style visuals~[2]
      \item Lock-and-key ability gating~[4]. The key to the ``door'' is you, noticing.

\begin{frame}{Methods}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \column{0.52\textwidth}
    \footnotesize
    \textbf{North star:} the character controller. Hit-stun and i-frames millisecond-tuned; expressive moves chain into each other; approachable, with a high skill ceiling.
    Everything flows from that!

    \begin{description}[Notice]
      \item[Burrow] Dive into any surface and swim through it; pop out with momentum.
      \item[Ride] Burrow lines are express tunnels. Some invert the room's geometry.
      \item[Move] Ropes, conveyors, contraptions, bouncers.
      \item[Fight] Readable combat and bosses which test your grasp of the expressive moveset.
      \item[Notice] Knowledge gates: the way forward is on screen before you understand it.
    \end{description}

    \textbf{Apparatus:} a custom engine and in-game editor for that ``and now for something (mostly) different'' vibe

\begin{frame}{Materials}
  \begin{columns}[c]
    \column{0.55\textwidth}
    \small
    The datacenter is alive. Its surfaces are painted by hand and grown by
    \alert{neural cellular automata}: grids of tiny cells, each running the same small learned rule.

    \medskip
    Nobody animates them. Hit one, and it has to heal. The cells are part of the gameplay: swim through them, find a way to use them against your foes...

    \medskip
    \textbf{Instruments:} live textures, dynamic lighting, shaders everywhere.

    \medskip
    \footnotesize
    Trained in-house on rented GPUs, building on Cells$^2$Pixels%
    \footnote[frame]{Pajouheshgar et al., \emph{From Cells to Pixels}, SIGGRAPH 2026. \weblink{https://cells2pixels.github.io/}}
    (used with the authors' permission).
